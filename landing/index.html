Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A focused feature set that does the basics exceptionally well.

01

Pick files before you download

Every torrent is resolved first and shown as a file tree, so you grab only what you want, with per-folder toggling.

02

Live stats, every second

Speed, progress, ETA and peers update in real time, with a speed chart for the selected torrent.

03

Every way to add

Magnet links, .torrent files, drag and drop, clipboard paste, or a double-click in your file manager. DHT works out of the box.

04

Lives in your tray

Close to tray, toggle with a click, launch with your system, and get notified when a download finishes.

05

Light on resources

Built with Rust and Tauri, so it starts instantly and sips memory instead of shipping a whole browser.

06

Dark, light, your way

Switch themes, zoom the interface, sort and multi-select, and set speed limits to match your connection.
